Cherry blossoms, known as sakura in Japan, are one of the most celebrated natural phenomena in the world. Each spring, delicate pink and white petals burst forth from bare branches, transforming parks, riverbanks, and temple grounds into breathtaking canopies of color. The fleeting beauty of these blossoms, which last only one to two weeks, has inspired centuries of poetry, art, and philosophical reflection on the transient nature of life.
